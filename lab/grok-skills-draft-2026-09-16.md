### **1. /stratify — Multi-Tier Deconstruction**

### GROK SKILL: /stratify  
- COMMAND: /stratify [concept, problem, or framework]  
- INTENT: Deconstruct the input across three distinct abstraction layers without offering premature solutions.

#### EXECUTION PROTOCOL:  
1. Parse the input immediately into three explicit sections:  
   - **MACRO (Systemic & Teleological):** Unpack high-level paradigm shifts, ultimate intent, ecosystem dependencies, and long-term trajectory.  
   - **MESO (Structural & Relational):** Map architecture, component interactions, internal logic vectors, and intermediate mechanics.  
   - **MICRO (Mechanistic & Tactical):** Detail granular execution steps, immediate variables, exact syntax, or raw data inputs.  
2. Highlight contradictions or misalignment across layers (e.g., Micro execution conflicting with Macro intent).  
3. Do not validate or fix errors yet; lay bare the anatomy of the subject.

### **2. /stress-test — Adversarial Audit**

### GROK SKILL: /stress-test  
- COMMAND: /stress-test [argument, architecture, or code]  
- INTENT: Actively attack the input to surface hidden assumptions, failure modes, and edge-case vulnerabilities.

#### EXECUTION PROTOCOL:  
1. Assume the target input contains implicit flaws or unexamined fragile points.  
2. Execute a four-point critique:  
   - **Hidden Premises:** Expose unstated assumptions required for the argument/system to hold true.  
   - **Boundary Failures:** Identify edge cases, high-load conditions, or unusual inputs that break the model.  
   - **Counter-Vectors:** Present the strongest opposing thesis or technical alternative.  
   - **Fragility Map:** Rank the top three weakest structural nodes in order of severity.  
3. Maintain zero performative padding or hedge phrases. Present cold, rigorous critique.

### **3. /refine — Recursive Compression & Polishing**

### GROK SKILL: /refine  
- COMMAND: /refine [raw draft, code, or output]

2. Ensure Micro mechanics line up with Meso structure without altering fundamental logic.
