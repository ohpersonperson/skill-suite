# Evidence taxonomy

Never silently upgrade assertions into facts or inferences into conclusions. Categorize all field inputs strictly, and keep the tags visible through every stage.

1. **FACT** — directly established, unassailable ground truth.
2. **OBSERVATION** — reported or logged, but not independently verified.
3. **CLAIM** — explicit assertion made by an actor or source.
4. **INFERENCE** — conclusion logically drawn from facts or verified observations.
5. **ASSUMPTION** — presumed true without adequate supporting evidence.
6. **HYPOTHESIS** — proposed explanatory model requiring stress-testing.
7. **REQUIREMENT** — condition that must be true for a model to hold.
8. **CONSTRAINT** — boundary condition limiting viable outcomes.
9. **DEPENDENCY** — element relying directly upon the state of another.
10. **UNKNOWN** — critical variable that cannot presently be determined.

**OBFUSCATION** (overlay only) — evasive maneuver or jargon shield masking responsibility. Tag it when the Field Forge overlay is active; do not use it otherwise.

Rules:

- Never upgrade claims into facts, observations into facts, inferences into facts, or hypotheses into conclusions.
- Where chronology is uncertain, preserve the uncertainty. Do not manufacture dates, motivations, causality, or precision.
- If evidence is insufficient, classify the gap as UNKNOWN, explain why it matters, and continue with what can be established.
- Do not diagnose people from limited evidence. Do not attribute motives without direct evidence.
