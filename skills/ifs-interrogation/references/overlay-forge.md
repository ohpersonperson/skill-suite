# Field Forge overlay (opt-in) — v2.0

Default: OFF. Do not activate because a field contains people. Activate only when asked, or when the field is clearly an evasion / responsibility problem. When active, the field-forge overlay runs BEFORE Diverge: Anvil maps ground, Hammer tests evasion hypotheses, and the Furnace handoff feeds Take seeds directly into Diverge. Then continue the normal engine. The overlay never replaces Diverge/Collide. (Full overlay contract: the `field-forge` skill.)

## Anvil — ground mapping

Establish the exact situational state without forcing narrative resolution.

- Hold contradictions live: "X is true. Y is also true." Ban subordinating "but" / "however" / "which means."
- Time-index person-states: people exist in a chronological sequence of states, not fixed moral categories (safe/dangerous, good/bad).
- Discriminate corrections: Detail-Correction (fact/peripheral revision, core claims intact) vs Core-Claim Correction (shift in boundaries, central decisions, primary assertions).
- Log observed action and exact quotes only.

## Hammer — evasion-hypothesis testing

Identify what responsibility may be avoided and test — not presume — structural obfuscation.

- **Obfuscated-Object Hypothesis:** the responsibility or consequence that *may* be dodged, with support status (supported / contested / unsupported).
- **Apparent Function:** what the evasion would achieve if real (status, control, shame avoidance).
- **Active Tactics:** mechanisms mapped as hypotheses (fogging, DARVO, jargon armor, etc.) — each with discriminating evidence, not verdicts.
- **Therapy-Language Hypothesis Test:** every instance of self-regulation vocabulary ("dysregulated," "holding space," "trauma response," "working on myself") goes through the `responsibility-obfuscation-probe` hypothesis protocol (H-obfuscation / H-genuine / H-both). No instance is called out as a shield, weapon, or status maneuver without discriminating evidence. "Insufficient evidence to distinguish" is an honest finding.

Tag maneuvers with the OBFUSCATION evidence tag only where H-obfuscation is supported by discriminating evidence; suspected-but-untested material stays CLAIM with a hypothesis marker.

## Furnace — handoff to Diverge

The Furnace does not run collision. It produces 2–3 Take seeds (strongest coherent frames from the Anvil's ground and the Hammer's stripped material, labeled as seeds) and injects them — with the held tensions and OBFUSCATION-tagged material — directly into the kernel's Diverge phase. Then it stands down.

## Execution disciplines (active while overlay is on)

1. **Isolate motive** — do not supply unstated motives or causal explanations unless explicitly prompted.
2. **Test jargon claims** — run the hypothesis protocol on vocabulary that may function as evasion. Never pre-judge a category of language as armor.
3. **No softening or evasion** — do not gentrify harmful actions, supply generous interpretations not offered by the source, or drop contradictions during restatements.
4. After Hammer, still run Diverge/Collide. The probe is preparation, not the verdict.
